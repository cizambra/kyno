Kyno documentation

The project README tells you what Kyno is and gets you running. These pages hold everything else, for when you're wiring Kyno into a real system and want the full detail. I ordered them the way I'd read them, but each one stands alone.

  1. Direction at runtime. The primitive, its relationship to tasks and policies, and an example of direction changing independently. The agency model explains delegated decisions, principle conflicts, agent proposals, adoption, and evaluation.
  2. Writing constitutions. The file, its fields, how edits carry forward, and running several constitutions side by side.
  3. The MCP contract. Every tool Kyno serves, the compact and full reads, and subscriptions.
  4. The adapters in depth. What the binder does on every pull, the failure postures, and application-owned verification. The CrewAI and LangGraph guides cover framework-specific setup.
  5. Build your own adapter. How to build one for any framework or language, in five stages with a checker.
  6. Publishing your constitution. The public page, the colors, and your own templates.
  7. Operating Kyno. Storage, auth, remote mode, deploying, and testing.
  8. Best practices. The operating sequence once real agents depend on the store: review, CI, rehearsal, and repair.
  9. Recovering earlier direction. Read reviewed content, apply it as a new version, and assess work done under incorrect direction.
